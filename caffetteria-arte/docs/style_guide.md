# Style guide (from the Pinterest reference "Creative Instagram Motion Graphics Reel for Marketing")

Reference: ref/pin_reference.mp4 (720x1280, 77.6 s, 22 shots, median shot 2.8 s, 2.7 cuts / 10 s).

## Take from the reference (motion + composition)
- Kinetic type pairs: a small word set against a huge bold word in the accent ("they / THINK", "Before / WE RUN").
  Words enter one at a time: slide from a side, unmask behind a coloured bar, or type on.
- Backgrounds alternate: near-black with a faint 80 px grid and vignette / light grey paper with soft leaf shadows.
- Thin white (on dark) or grey (on light) swoosh curves cross the frame and are drawn on.
- One saturated accent used for type, highlight bars, duotone on cut-out photos, small icons.
- A phone mockup is the recurring hero: the real Instagram profile, zoom to details, a hand/tap, a follow button.
- Foreground floating objects add depth (in the reference: bills and leaves; here: coffee beans, leaves).
- End card: a FOLLOW pill above the logo on light paper.

## Keep from the brand
- Logo (cream disc, brown script), the café's own words (bio: caffè, colazioni, pranzi veloci, aperitivi; arte, libri, relax; punto d'incontro).
- Accent: a warm red (#C8322B), which sits with coffee brown and cream; two faces: Inter (UI, heavy for the big words) + Playfair (italic for the small words).
